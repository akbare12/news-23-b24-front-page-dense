A one-page newspaper front page summing up 23 November 2026 in one image: 31 stories across 6 regions.

**The three big stories:** the AI rout hits Asia (Kospi −3.3%), Guterres calls for AI rules at the Bridgetown summit, and the US lead over China hits a record low in the Asia Power Index 2026.

**Inside:** a 10-number strip, 3 hero scenes, 10 more stories, a world map with numbered pins, an ALSO list, and a DATE CHECK box that keeps out stories dated 22 November or earlier.

Scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs. Sources: Reuters, AP, Bloomberg, AFP, Yonhap, BBC, WSJ, NYT, ESPN, Lowy Institute.

Run `python3 main.py` to copy `entry.png` (1024×1024) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
