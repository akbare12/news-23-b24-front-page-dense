# THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

A dense front page summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026:
the AI rout that hit Asian markets (Kospi −3.3%), the UN chief's call to
regulate AI before it is too late, and the US lead over China falling to a
record low in the Asia Power Index — plus 28 more stories across 6 regions,
a world map with 31 numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK box that keeps every
story honestly dated.

`draw.py` (PIL, stdlib-only drawing) renders `entry.png` (1024×1024);
`main.py` copies it to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, verifying size and
sha256. Scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs.
